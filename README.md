# 👋 Hello, I'm Sikolia 🎶

## 🌟 Music Enthusiast | Frontend Developer | DAW Advocate | Tech Innovator

I’m on a mission to bridge **music** and **technology**, making **music education** accessible to everyone, everywhere. As a **Frontend Developer** and advocate for **Digital Audio Workstations (DAWs)**, I aim to create interactive, scalable platforms that bring music learning to the digital world.

![GitHub Stats Animation](https://tenor.com/bRPBh.gif)


With a background in **telecommunications engineering**, I use my skills to build **web solutions** that transform education through **innovative tech**.

---

## 🚀 What I’m Working On:
- **Frontend Development** using **HTML**, **CSS**, **JavaScript**, and **React** to craft responsive, user-friendly websites and apps.
- Building **scalable platforms** for **Vivace Music School**, transforming music education and providing global access to quality learning.
- Integrating **Digital Audio Workstations (DAWs)** into platforms to teach **music production**, allowing students to create, mix, and experiment with sound.
- Empowering students to create with tools like **Ableton Live**, **FL Studio**, and **Logic Pro X**, while pushing for more interactive learning experiences.
